Worry Parking

Privacy Policy

Effective 19 September 2026

In short: Worry Parking does not collect personal data.

  • There is no account or sign-in, and no servers run by the developer.
  • The worries you write and your history are stored only on your device.
  • The app uses no third-party SDKs for ads, analytics, or tracking.

1. Information stored on your device

To make the app work, the following is stored only on your iPhone. The developer cannot access it, and it is never sent anywhere.

DataPurpose
Worry text, level (1–5), spot numberShowing tickets, exit gate review
Parked and exit times, review results (whether it happened, outcome, next step)Exit Log and insights
Experience feedback (level before and after parking, how it felt, an optional note)Showing it back to you under “Your experience” in the Exit Log
Worry Time setting, onboarding statusCalculating exit times, keeping your settings

If you have iCloud Backup or computer backups turned on, this data may be included in Apple's device backup along with other app data. Those backups are governed by Apple's privacy policy and are not accessible to the developer.

2. Notifications

Pickup reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They show only the spot number (for example, “P3 is ready for pickup”) and never include your worry text. You can turn them off at any time in Settings > Notifications.

3. Payments

The app has no purchases. The developer does not receive your name, email, or payment details.

4. Sharing with third parties

Because the developer does not collect personal data, none is shared with or processed by third parties.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Swipe left on an entry in the Exit Log to delete it.
  • Deleting the app removes all data it stored on your device.

6. Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

7. Health disclaimer

Worry Parking is a self-help tool. It is not a medical device and does not replace professional diagnosis or treatment.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Any change that would involve collecting data will be announced here before the app update ships.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, please open a GitHub issue.
