How it works

Worry postponement, a technique from cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT), built as a parking lot.

1

Write it down

Write the worry exactly as it sounds in your head and rate how loud it is, 1 to 5.

2

Park it

Parked worries stay blurred. If one drives back into your head, tell it: you already have a spot.

3

Exit gate

At Worry Time, give it 15 focused minutes. Check what really happened, then let it go, make a plan, or park it again.
