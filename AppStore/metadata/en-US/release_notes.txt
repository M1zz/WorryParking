Welcome to the first version of Worry Parking.

Write down the worry that keeps circling and park it. It comes back at your Worry Time, and until then you don't have to carry it.

When you start, the app explains why worry postponement works, with the research behind it.
Try it right away by parking one real worry and seeing how its level changes before and after.
Share how it felt after your first parking and your first exit. Your answers stay on this iPhone and show up under Your experience in the Exit Log.
Read Why it works again anytime from Settings.
Reminder permission is asked once, when you pick your Worry Time.
